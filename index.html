COMPANY DESCRIPTION

Clarinda Park Ventures Limited is a private Irish holding company, registered with the Companies Registration Office under number 825388. It owns the entire share capital of two Irish software companies: ComplyHQ Tech Limited, which develops ComplyHQ, and Ardwright Limited, which develops Practice Manager.

WHAT WE DO
01

Equity Ownership

Clarinda Park Ventures holds 100 per cent of the shares in each of its two subsidiaries, ComplyHQ Tech Limited and Ardwright Limited.

02

Governance

The company is governed jointly by its two shareholders, each holding 50 per cent.

03

Long-Term View

The company takes a patient, long-term approach to what it holds, rather than seeking short-term returns.
