What it does

The practice side of an accountancy firm, in one system

Most firms run the business of the practice across a calendar, a timesheet, a billing tool, a shared drive and a lot of email. Practice Manager brings those together, so the work, the hours and the fees all hang off the same client record.

Jobs and deadlines

Every piece of client work is a job with an owner, a reviewer and a due date. Recurring work such as year-end accounts and tax returns is scheduled once and generated each period.

  • Personal work queues and a team view of who has what
  • Deadline calendar with reminders and escalation
  • Reusable job templates with stages and checklists

Time recording

Record time against the client and job it belongs to, with a running timer or a weekly timesheet. Time flows straight into work in progress, ready to bill.

  • Timer and daily or weekly timesheets
  • Staff rates and billable or non-billable time
  • Timesheet submission and approval

Fee billing

Turn recorded time into a fee note in a few clicks, with the draft reviewed and approved before it goes to the client. See what each job has earned and what is still to bill.

  • Bills and credit notes raised from work in progress
  • Draft, approve and issue, with a full history
  • Outstanding fees and job profitability by client

Client records and correspondence

One record for each client and contact, with their services, team and history. Emails, letters, call notes and documents are filed against the client, so anyone in the firm can pick up where a colleague left off.

  • Clients, contacts and the relationships between them
  • Correspondence and documents filed in context
  • A timeline of everything that has happened on a client
How it is built

Designed around how a practice actually runs

01

Client first

Every job, timesheet, fee note and letter is reachable from the client it belongs to.

02

Nothing changes silently

Bills move from draft to approved to issued. Changes are made by reversal, and every action is recorded in an audit trail.

03

Your data, held in the EU

Hosted in the European Union, with access controlled per firm and per user, and built for GDPR from the start.

About

Who we are

Ardwright Limited develops Practice Manager, cloud software that accountancy firms use to run their own practice: tracking jobs and deadlines, recording time, billing fees, and managing client records and correspondence.

It will be sold by subscription to accountancy firms in Ireland, with the UK as a later market. The company is newly incorporated and pre-revenue.

Ardwright Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Clarinda Park Ventures Limited, an Irish company based in Dún Laoghaire, Co. Dublin.

Practice Manager is software. Ardwright Limited does not hold client money and does not provide financial services.
